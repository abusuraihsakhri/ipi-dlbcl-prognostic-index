    Methods and references

    Five factors contribute one point each: age >60, LDH above ULN, ECOG ≥2, Ann Arbor III/IV, and >1 extranodal site.

    Original IPI: 0–1 low; 2 low-intermediate; 3 high-intermediate; 4–5 high. R-IPI: 0 very good; 1–2 good; 3–5 poor.

    International NHL Prognostic Factors Project, NEJM (1993) · Sehn et al., Blood (2007)
